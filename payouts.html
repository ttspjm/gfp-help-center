Rewards and payouts

Payouts

When you can request a payout, payout caps and how payouts affect your account.

How payouts work

In the funded stage you can request a payout every 14 days on GOAT or every 10 days on Premium. You keep 80% of your profit, paid out through Rise or in crypto.

The 80% split is standard. The optional profit split add-on raises it to 100%, so you keep all of your profit. It costs 20% on top of the evaluation price and can only be added at checkout, not later.

Before each payout

  • You've reached your payout date.
  • Your identity verification (KYC) is approved. You only do this once.
  • You have the profitable days for the current payout cycle: 4 on GOAT or 5 on Premium. Each one needs closed profit of at least 0.5% of your starting balance ($50 on $10K, $125 on $25K, $250 on $50K, $500 on $100K).
  • You meet the 30% consistency rule: no single trading day is more than 30% of the profit in the cycle.
  • You have at least the $100 minimum in closed profit.
  • All your positions are closed.

You can't request a payout on a Saturday or Sunday.

Payout cycles

Every approved payout starts a new payout cycle. The profitable days and the consistency rule start again and only count the new cycle.

Payout caps

The cap is 3% of your starting balance on your 1st and 2nd payouts and 4% from your 3rd, on GOAT and Premium. It applies to your profit before the split. On a $25K account with the 80% split, you receive up to $600 on your 1st and 2nd payouts and up to $800 after that. Giveaway accounts have a lifetime payout cap of $1,000. See Payout caps for every size.

On GOAT, your first payout locks your maximum drawdown floor at your starting balance. You choose how much to withdraw. See How payouts affect your drawdown.

Starter has a single payout, and the account closes afterwards. See Starter model rules.

Payout caps

Each payout has a cap on how much profit you can withdraw. The caps are the same on GOAT and Premium. The cap applies to your profit before the profit split, and your split is then paid on the capped amount.

With the 100% add-on you receive the full capped amount.

Example

On a $25K account you have $1,200 in closed profit on your first payout. The cap is $750, so you can withdraw $750 of it. With the 80% split you receive $600. With the 100% add-on you receive the full $750.

The other $450 stays in your account.

Giveaway accounts

Giveaway accounts have a lifetime payout cap of $1,000, counted across all payout cycles.

Starter

Starter has a single payout with an 80% profit split, and the account closes afterwards. See Starter model rules.

How payouts affect your drawdown

GOAT

Your first payout locks your maximum drawdown floor at your starting balance, if it isn't there already. From then on, if your equity reaches or falls below your starting balance, the account is breached.

You choose how much to withdraw. What you leave in the account is what sits above the floor.

Example: on a $25K account your equity is $25,600 and the floor locks at $25,000. A $600 payout leaves your equity at $25,000, on the floor, and the account closes. A $400 payout leaves your equity at $25,200, which is $200 above the floor.

Premium

Your floor stays fixed 5% below your starting balance, so a payout doesn't move it.

Example: on a $25K account your floor is $23,750 before and after a payout.

Starter

Your account closes after your single payout, so the floor no longer matters.
