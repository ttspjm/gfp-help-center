Rewards and payouts

Payouts

When you can request a payout, payout caps and how payouts affect your account.

How payouts work

In the funded stage you can request a payout every 14 days. You keep 80% of your profit, paid out through Rise or in crypto.

The 80% split is standard. The optional profit split add-on raises it to 100%, so you keep all of your profit. It costs 20% on top of the evaluation price and can only be added at checkout, not later.

Before each payout

  • Your identity verification (KYC) must be approved. You only do this once.
  • You need 3 qualifying profit days in the current payout cycle: days where your realized profit is at least 0.5% of your starting balance ($125 on $25K, $250 on $50K, $500 on $100K).

Payout cycles

Every approved payout starts a new payout cycle. Your withdrawable profit resets to $0, and your equity after the payout becomes your new baseline.

The consistency rule resets with each payout too. No single trading day can make up more than 35% of the profit you make in the new cycle.

If your equity falls below your baseline, new profit first rebuilds it. Withdrawable profit only starts building again once your equity is back above the baseline. The baseline works like a high-water mark.

Minimum payout

The minimum payout is $100.

Payout caps

The cap per payout cycle is $750 on $25K, $1,250 on $50K and $2,500 on $100K. It applies to your profit before the split, so with the 80% split you receive up to $600, $1,000 or $2,000 per cycle. See Payout caps for what happens to profit above the cap.

Requesting a payout locks your maximum drawdown floor at your starting balance. You choose how much to withdraw, so you can leave some profit in the account as a buffer. See How payouts affect your drawdown before you request one.

Payout caps

Each payout cycle has a cap on how much profit you can withdraw. The cap applies to your profit before the profit split. Your split is then paid on the capped amount.

Account sizePayout cap per cycleYou receive (80% split)You receive (100% add-on)
$25K$750$600$750
$50K$1,250$1,000$1,250
$100K$2,500$2,000$2,500

What happens to profit above the cap

Profit above the cap stays in your funded account as equity. It does not carry forward as withdrawable profit into the next payout cycle.

The extra equity still gives you more room against drawdown and future losses.

Every approved payout starts a new cycle, and your withdrawable profit resets to $0.

Example

On a $25K account you make $2,000 profit in a cycle. The cap is $750, so you can withdraw $750 of it. With the 80% split you receive $600. With the 100% add-on you receive the full $750.

The other $1,250 stays in your account as equity. You can't withdraw it.

How payouts affect your drawdown

Requesting a payout permanently locks your maximum drawdown floor at your starting balance.

After your first payout request, if your account equity reaches or falls below your starting balance, the account is breached.

Keep a buffer. If you withdraw all of your profit, your equity is left at the floor and the account is closed.

How much to leave in the account

There's no fixed amount. You choose how much to withdraw. A partial payout leaves some profit in the account as a buffer above the floor, so you can keep trading.

Example

On a $25K account, your floor locks at $25,000 once you request a payout. From then on your equity has to stay above $25,000.
