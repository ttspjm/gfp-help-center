What is Goat Funded Predictions?

Goat Funded Predictions is Goat Funded Trader's evaluation for prediction markets. You trade shares on the outcome of events, pass a 1-Step Evaluation, and then trade a funded account with payouts every 14 days. You keep 80% of your profit, or 100% with the optional profit split add-on.

Every market and price comes from Kalshi. You trade with simulated capital, so your own money is never at risk on a position. There are no trading fees.

How to get started

  1. Log in to your Goat Funded Trader dashboard. Goat Funded Predictions uses the same login, so you don't need a new account.
  2. Switch to Predictions using the product selector in your dashboard.
  3. Choose an account size and buy your evaluation. If you want the 100% profit split, add it at checkout.

The path to a payout

  3. Trade your funded account and request payouts every 14 days, keeping 80% of your profit, or 100% with the add-on.

Do I need to verify my identity?

Yes. Every trader completes identity verification (KYC) once, before moving to the funded stage. Payouts can't be released until your KYC is approved.

How it works

We use Sumsub, a third-party identity verification provider. You'll be asked to submit a valid government-issued photo ID and complete a quick liveness check.

When to do it

You can complete KYC from your account settings once you pass your evaluation and move into the funded review period. Most submissions are reviewed within 24 to 48 hours.

Why we ask

Verification is required to comply with know-your-customer and anti-money-laundering regulations, and to protect against fraud.

Can I have more than one account?

Each person can have only one Goat Funded login. Under that login you can buy and trade as many evaluations as you like at the same time.

Funded capital cap

You can hold up to $300,000 in funded capital across all your active funded accounts, in any mix of sizes. For example, three $100K accounts, or six $50K accounts.

Once you're at the cap, a newly passed evaluation can't be funded until some of your funded capital frees up.

The prohibited trading practices apply across all of your accounts. For example, you can't take opposite positions in two of your accounts to make sure one of them passes.

Which countries are restricted?

If you live in or are located in one of these countries, you can't create an account, buy an evaluation or receive payouts:

Afghanistan, Belarus, Bolivia, Bosnia, Central African Republic, Chile, Cuba, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Hong Kong, Iran, Israel, Jordan, Laos, Lebanon, Libya, Mali, Myanmar, North Korea, Papua New Guinea, Russia, Senegal, Singapore, Somalia, South Korea, South Sudan, Sudan, Syria, Togo, Ukraine, Venezuela, Yemen, Zimbabwe.

This list can change at any time to stay compliant with laws and sanctions. By buying an evaluation, you confirm you're not a resident of, or located in, any of these countries.

Payment methods and checkout

You can pay for an evaluation by card, digital wallet or crypto. Checkout offers four options:

  • Credit / Debit card: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay.
  • Crypto / Card / PayPal / Kora / AstroPay: Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, USDC, PayPal, Kora, Skrill and AstroPay.
  • Credit / Debit card (alternative processor): Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay. Use this if your card was declined on the first option.
  • Crypto: Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, USDC.

Every purchase is a one-time payment. Nothing renews or charges your card again without your permission. We don't store your full card details.

My payment was declined

  1. Try the alternative card processor.
  2. Check your bank hasn't blocked the payment. Many banks decline first payments to prop firms, and a quick call or in-app approval usually fixes it.
  3. Check the billing details match your card exactly, including the postcode.
  4. Paying with American Express? Use the first card option.
  5. Paying in crypto? Send the exact amount on the correct network.

Using someone else's card

You can only use payment methods you're authorised to use. We may ask for identity verification or proof that the card is yours if a payment looks suspicious, and some payments are held for a manual check before the evaluation is activated.

Chargebacks

If something went wrong with a purchase, contact support first. A chargeback filed without contacting us, or one we find fraudulent, can lead to account suspension, cancelled evaluations and funded accounts, and held payouts while it's investigated.

Is the evaluation fee refundable?

You can get a refund only if your evaluation is untouched: no trades placed and the balance unchanged since it was issued. Once you've placed a trade, the fee isn't refundable.

An evaluation that fails, including one that runs out of time, isn't refunded. The fee also isn't refunded later with a payout.

Account sizes and prices

Goat Funded Predictions has one evaluation, the 1-Step Evaluation, in three account sizes. The rules are the same for every size. The size sets your price, your share limit per event, your profit target in dollars and your payout cap.

The optional 100% profit split add-on costs 20% on top of the evaluation price. You can only add it at checkout, when you buy the evaluation.

How the 1-Step Evaluation works

The 1-Step Evaluation has a single phase. Pass it once and you move on to the funded stage.

How to pass

Reach the 8% profit target while staying within these rules:

  • Maximum drawdown of 3.5%, trailing intraday.
  • Daily drawdown of 2% of your balance at the start of the day.
  • Consistency rule of 35%.
  • No more than 30 open positions at once.
  • No more shares per event than your account size allows.
  • Only events priced at 0.85 or below.
  • No bots or automated tools.

There is no minimum number of trading days in the evaluation. You pass as soon as you hit the profit target within the rules.

Time limit

You have 120 days to pass the evaluation. If time runs out before you pass, the account fails and the fee isn't refunded.

After you pass

Once you pass, your account moves into the funded review period. Complete identity verification (KYC) from your account settings. Most submissions are reviewed within 24 to 48 hours. KYC is required before you move to the funded stage.

In the funded stage you can request payouts every 14 days and keep 80% of your profit, or 100% with the optional profit split add-on.
