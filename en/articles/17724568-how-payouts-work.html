How payouts work

Updated 27 October 2026

In the funded stage you can request a payout every 14 days. You keep 80% of your profit, paid out through Rise or in crypto.

The 80% split is standard. The optional profit split add-on raises it to 100%, so you keep all of your profit. It costs 20% on top of the evaluation price and can only be added at checkout, not later.

Before each payout

  • Your identity verification (KYC) must be approved. You only do this once.
  • You need 3 qualifying profit days in the current payout cycle: days where your realized profit is at least 0.5% of your starting balance ($125 on $25K, $250 on $50K, $500 on $100K).

Payout cycles

Every approved payout starts a new payout cycle. Your withdrawable profit resets to $0, and your equity after the payout becomes your new baseline.

The consistency rule resets with each payout too. No single trading day can make up more than 35% of the profit you make in the new cycle.

If your equity falls below your baseline, new profit first rebuilds it. Withdrawable profit only starts building again once your equity is back above the baseline. The baseline works like a high-water mark.

Minimum payout

The minimum payout is $100.

Payout caps

The cap per payout cycle is $750 on $25K, $1,250 on $50K and $2,500 on $100K. It applies to your share, after the profit split. See Payout caps for what happens to profit above the cap.

Requesting a payout locks your maximum drawdown floor at your starting balance. You choose how much to withdraw, so you can leave some profit in the account as a buffer. See How payouts affect your drawdown before you request one.
