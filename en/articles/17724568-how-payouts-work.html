How payouts work

Updated 5 November 2026

In the funded stage you can request a payout every 14 days on GOAT or every 10 days on Premium. You keep 80% of your profit, paid out through Rise or in crypto.

The 80% split is standard. The optional profit split add-on raises it to 100%, so you keep all of your profit. It costs 20% on top of the evaluation price and can only be added at checkout, not later.

Before each payout

  • You've reached your payout date.
  • Your identity verification (KYC) is approved. You only do this once.
  • You have the profitable days for the current payout cycle: 4 on GOAT or 5 on Premium. Each one needs closed profit of at least 0.5% of your starting balance ($50 on $10K, $125 on $25K, $250 on $50K, $500 on $100K).
  • You meet the 30% consistency rule: no single trading day is more than 30% of the profit in the cycle.
  • You have at least the $100 minimum in closed profit.
  • All your positions are closed.

You can't request a payout on a Saturday or Sunday.

Payout cycles

Every approved payout starts a new payout cycle. The profitable days and the consistency rule start again and only count the new cycle.

Payout caps

The cap is 3% of your starting balance on your 1st and 2nd payouts and 4% from your 3rd, on GOAT and Premium. It applies to your profit before the split. On a $25K account with the 80% split, you receive up to $600 on your 1st and 2nd payouts and up to $800 after that. Giveaway accounts have a lifetime payout cap of $1,000. See Payout caps for every size.

On GOAT, your first payout locks your maximum drawdown floor at your starting balance. You choose how much to withdraw. See How payouts affect your drawdown.

Rookie has a single payout, and the account closes afterwards. See Rookie model rules.
