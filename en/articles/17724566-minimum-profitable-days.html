On Starter you need 5 profitable days before your payout.

What counts as a profitable day

A day counts when it closes with at least 0.5% of your starting balance in closed profit.

Account sizeClosed profit needed
$10K$50
$25K$125
$50K$250
$100K$500

On Starter that's $12.50.

The days don't have to be consecutive, and weekends count. The trading day resets at 5:00 PM ET (New York time).

After a payout

The count starts again after every payout, so you need the full number of days in each payout cycle.
