Position and share limits

Updated 24 October 2026

Open positions

You can hold up to 30 open positions at once across your account.

The limit counts each distinct market and side you hold, and it is the same for every account size. It applies to both evaluation and funded accounts.

Once you have 30 open positions, close one before you open a new one.
