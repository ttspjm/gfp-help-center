Position and share limits

Updated 14 November 2026

Open positions

You can hold up to 30 open positions at once across your account.

The limit counts each distinct market and side you hold, and it is the same for every account size. It applies to both evaluation and funded accounts.

Once you have 30 open positions, close one before you open a new one.

On Starter the limit is 125 shares per event.

This is a ceiling, so you can always trade fewer shares.

What counts as one event

An event means all the Kalshi markets on the same real-world fact, such as the same game, election or data release. Kalshi can split one game into several markets, like the winner, the spread, the total and props. The limit counts all of them together, YES and NO shares combined.

Example: 600 shares on the winner of a game plus 400 on the spread of the same game is 1,000 shares on one event. That's the full limit on a $25K GOAT account.
