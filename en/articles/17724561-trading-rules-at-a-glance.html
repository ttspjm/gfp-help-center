Consistency rule

No single trading day can make up more than 30% of your total profit for the period. On GOAT this applies in the evaluation and the funded stage. On Premium it applies in the funded stage only.

Going over it doesn't close the account. You can't pass or request a payout until your best day is within 30%, and you can keep trading until it is.

In the funded stage it resets with every approved payout and only counts the profit you make in the new payout cycle.

Example: on a $25K GOAT evaluation, the $2,000 profit target means no single day can contribute more than $600. If your best day is $700, you pass once your total profit reaches $2,334.
