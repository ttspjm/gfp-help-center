Account sizes and prices

Updated 5 November 2026

The size sets your price, your share limit per event, your profit target in dollars and your payout caps. The rules within a model are the same for every size.

The optional 100% profit split add-on costs 20% on top of the price. You can only add it at checkout, when you buy the evaluation.
