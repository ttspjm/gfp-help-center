How the 1-Step Evaluation works

Updated 5 November 2026

The 1-Step Evaluation has a single phase. Pass it once and you move on to the funded stage. GOAT and Premium both use it, with different rules.

Rookie has no evaluation.

How to pass

Reach the 8% profit target while staying within these rules:

RuleGOATPremium
Minimum profitable days45
Maximum drawdown4%, trailing intraday5%, static
Daily drawdown3%2.5%
Maximum floating loss1%None
Consistency rule30%None
Open positions3030
Max shares per event400, 1,000, 2,000 or 4,000, by size500, 1,250, 2,500 or 5,000, by size
Entry price5¢ to 85¢5¢ to 85¢
Bots and automated toolsNot allowedNot allowed

A profitable day is a day that closes with at least 0.5% of your starting balance in closed profit. See Minimum profitable days.

Time limit

You have 120 days to pass the evaluation. If time runs out before you pass, the account fails and the fee isn't refunded.

After you pass

Once you pass, your account moves into the funded review period. Complete identity verification (KYC) from your account settings. Most submissions are reviewed within 24 to 48 hours. KYC is required before you move to the funded stage.

In the funded stage you can request payouts every 14 days on GOAT or every 10 days on Premium, and keep 80% of your profit, or 100% with the optional profit split add-on.
