What is Goat Funded Predictions?

Updated 14 November 2026

Goat Funded Predictions is Goat Funded Trader's program for prediction markets. You trade shares on the outcome of events and get paid from the profit you make on a funded account. You keep 80% of your profit, or 100% with the optional profit split add-on on GOAT and Premium.

Every market and price comes from Kalshi. You can trade markets on politics, crypto, economics, sports and entertainment. You trade with simulated capital, so your own money is never at risk on a position. There are no trading fees.

How to get started

  1. Log in to your Goat Funded Trader dashboard. Goat Funded Predictions uses the same login, so you don't need a new account.
  2. Switch to Predictions using the product selector in your dashboard.
  3. Choose an account and a size, then buy it. If you want the 100% profit split, add it at checkout.

The path to a payout

  1. Pass the 1-Step Evaluation by reaching the 8% profit target within the rules, with at least 4 profitable days on GOAT or 5 on Premium. You have 120 days.
  2. Verify your identity (KYC) during the funded review period.
  3. Trade your funded account and request payouts every 14 days on GOAT or every 10 days on Premium, keeping 80% of your profit, or 100% with the add-on.

Starter works differently. You trade one account, and once you reach the 8% profit target within the rules you can request a single payout from it. See Starter model rules.
