What happens if I fail my evaluation?

Updated 27 October 2026

Your evaluation fails if you:

  • go below your maximum drawdown floor,
  • lose more than 2% of your balance at the start of the day in one trading day, or
  • run out of time before reaching the profit target (120 days).

A failed evaluation can't be restarted and its fee isn't refunded. To try again, buy a new evaluation.
