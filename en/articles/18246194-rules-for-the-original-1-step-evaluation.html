Rules for the original 1-Step Evaluation

Updated 5 November 2026

If you bought a $25K, $50K or $100K evaluation at the original prices of $79, $149 or $249, your account keeps the rules it was sold with, shown here. All other articles describe GOAT and Premium.

RuleLimit
Profit target8% (evaluation)
Maximum drawdown3.5%, trailing intraday. Locks at your starting balance, and at your first payout request.
Daily drawdown2%. Resets at 5:00 PM ET.
Consistency rule35%, evaluation and funded
Evaluation time limit120 days
Minimum profitable daysNone in the evaluation. 3 before each payout, each with at least 0.5% profit.
Open positions30 at once
Max shares per event1,250 ($25K), 2,500 ($50K), 4,000 ($100K)
Entry price85¢ or below
Funded inactivity15 days
PayoutsEvery 14 days, via Rise or crypto
Payout cap per cycle$750 ($25K), $1,250 ($50K), $2,500 ($100K), before the split
Minimum payout$100
Profit split80%, or 100% with the add-on bought at checkout

Profit above the cap

Profit above the cap stays in your funded account as equity. It does not carry forward as withdrawable profit into the next payout cycle.

Payout cycles

Every approved payout starts a new payout cycle. Your withdrawable profit resets to $0, and your equity after the payout becomes your new baseline. If your equity falls below your baseline, new profit first rebuilds it.
