Maximum drawdown

Updated 29 October 2026

Your maximum drawdown is 3.5%, trailing intraday. It sets the floor your account must stay above.

Where your floor starts

Account sizeStarting floor
$25K$24,125
$50K$48,250
$100K$96,500

How it trails

The floor follows your equity in real time, including open positions. When your equity reaches a new high, the floor moves up and stays 3.5% of your starting balance below that high. That's $875 on $25K, $1,750 on $50K and $3,500 on $100K. The floor never moves down.

Example: on a $25K account your floor starts at $24,125. During the day your open positions take your equity to $25,400, so the floor moves up to $24,525. The positions then drop back and you end the day at $25,100. Your floor stays at $24,525.

When it locks

Once the floor reaches your starting balance, it stops trailing and stays at your starting balance.

Requesting a payout also locks the floor at your starting balance, permanently. After your first payout request, if your account equity reaches or falls below your starting balance, the account is breached.

Keep a buffer. Once the floor is at your starting balance, withdrawing all of your profit leaves nothing above the floor, and the account is closed. Request a partial payout to keep some profit in the account.
