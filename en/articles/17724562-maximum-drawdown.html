Maximum drawdown

Updated 14 November 2026

Your maximum drawdown sets the floor your account must stay above. It's 4%, trailing intraday on GOAT and 5%, static on Premium.

Where your floor starts

$10K$9,600$9,500
$25K$24,000$23,750
$50K$48,000$47,500
$100K$96,000$95,000

GOAT: how it trails

The floor follows your equity in real time, including open positions. When your equity reaches a new high, the floor moves up and stays 4% of your starting balance below that high. That's $400 on $10K, $1,000 on $25K, $2,000 on $50K and $4,000 on $100K. The floor never moves down.

Example: on a $25K account your floor starts at $24,000. During the day your open positions take your equity to $25,400, so the floor moves up to $24,400. The positions then drop back and you end the day at $25,100. Your floor stays at $24,400.

GOAT: when it locks

Once your equity reaches 4% above your starting balance, the floor reaches your starting balance. It stops trailing and stays there.

In the funded stage, your first payout also locks the floor at your starting balance. From then on, if your equity reaches or falls below your starting balance, the account is breached. See How payouts affect your drawdown.

Premium: a fixed floor

The floor is fixed 5% below your starting balance. It never moves up, so new equity highs and payouts don't change it.

Example: on a $25K account your floor is $23,750 from your first trade onwards.

Starter

Starter uses the same trailing method as GOAT at 4%, or $100. The floor starts at $2,400 and locks at $2,500 once it gets there.
