Starter model rules

Updated 14 November 2026

Starter is a $2,500 1-Step challenge for $10. You trade one account. Once you reach the 8% profit target ($200) within the rules, you can request a single payout from that account. The account closes after the payout.

Rules

RuleStarter
Account size$2,500
Price$10
Accounts per person1
Profit target8% ($200)
Maximum drawdown4% trailing intraday ($100). Locks at your starting balance once reached.
Daily drawdown2% ($50)
Maximum floating lossNone
Consistency rule30%
Minimum profitable days5, each with at least 0.5% closed profit ($12.50)
Open positions30
Max shares per event125
Entry priceUp to 85¢
Profit split80%, no add-on

Your payout

You can request your payout once you meet all of these:

  • You've reached the 8% profit target ($200).
  • You have 5 profitable days.
  • No single day is more than 30% of your total profit.
  • All your positions are closed.
  • Your identity verification (KYC) is approved.

You can't request a payout on a Saturday or Sunday. We review every Starter payout manually.

Example: if your best day is $90, your total profit has to reach $300 before you can request, because $90 is 30% of $300.

After the payout

The account closes after your payout. To keep trading you can buy a GOAT or Premium challenge.

Share limit example

125 shares per event is a ceiling, so you can trade fewer. At the maximum, a winning position bought at 50¢ pays $62.50. A losing position of 125 shares bought above 40¢ goes over the $50 daily limit.

Rookie accounts

Starter was called Rookie before. If you bought a Rookie account, it keeps the rules it was sold with: instant funded with no profit target, and a single payout from $75 in closed profit, capped at $150 before the split.
