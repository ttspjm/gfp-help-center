Daily drawdown

Updated 5 November 2026

Your daily drawdown is 3% on GOAT and 2.5% on Premium. If you lose more than that in one day, the account is breached.

When the day resets

The trading day resets at 5:00 PM ET (New York time). Your limit for the next day is measured from the higher of your balance or equity at that point.

Example

You start the day with $25,000, so you can lose up to $750 that day on GOAT or $625 on Premium. If you end the day at $25,600, your limit for the next day is $768 on GOAT or $640 on Premium.

On Rookie the daily drawdown is 2%, which is $50 at the $2,500 starting balance.
