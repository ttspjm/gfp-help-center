Entry price range

Updated 14 November 2026

On GOAT and Premium, your entry price must be between 5¢ and 85¢ when you open a position.

The range only applies when you open a position. A share bought at 60¢ can be held or sold at any price afterwards.

On Starter the maximum entry price is 85¢.
