Rookie model rules

Updated 5 November 2026

Rookie is a $2,500 instant funded account for $10. There's no evaluation. You start funded, you can take one payout, and then the account closes.

Rules

RuleRookie
Account size$2,500
Price$10
Accounts per person1
Maximum drawdown4% trailing intraday ($100). Locks at your starting balance once reached.
Daily drawdown2% ($50)
Consistency rule30%
Minimum profitable days5, each with at least 0.5% closed profit ($12.50)
Open positions30
Max shares per event125
Entry priceUp to 85¢
Inactivity30 days
Minimum payout3% ($75)
Payout cap6% ($150), before the split
Profit split80%, no add-on

Your payout

You can request your payout once you meet all of these:

  • You have 5 profitable days.
  • You have at least $75 in closed profit. Only closed profit counts.
  • No single day is more than 30% of your total profit.
  • All your positions are closed.
  • Your identity verification (KYC) is approved.

You can't request a payout on a Saturday or Sunday. We review every Rookie payout manually.

Example: if your best day is $60, your total profit must reach $200 before you can request. The payout is still capped at $150, so with the 80% split you receive $120.

After the payout

The account closes after your payout. Any profit above the $150 cap is forfeited. To keep trading you can buy another Rookie account, or a GOAT or Premium evaluation.

Share limit example

125 shares per event is a ceiling, so you can trade fewer. At the maximum, a winning position bought at 50¢ pays $62.50. A losing position of 125 shares bought above 40¢ goes over the $50 daily limit.
