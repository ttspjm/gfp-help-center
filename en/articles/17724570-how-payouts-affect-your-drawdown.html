How payouts affect your drawdown

Updated 24 October 2026

Requesting a payout permanently locks your maximum drawdown floor at your starting balance.

After your first payout request, if your account equity reaches or falls below your starting balance, the account is breached.

Keep a buffer. If you withdraw all of your profit, your equity is left at the floor and the account is closed.

Example

On a $25K account, your floor locks at $25,000 once you request a payout. From then on your equity has to stay above $25,000.
