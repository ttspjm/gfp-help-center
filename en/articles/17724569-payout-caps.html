Payout caps

Updated 24 October 2026

Each payout cycle has a cap on how much profit you can withdraw.

Account sizePayout cap per cycle
$25K$750
$50K$1,250
$100K$2,500

What happens to profit above the cap

Profit above the cap stays in your funded account as equity. It does not carry forward as withdrawable profit into the next payout cycle.

The extra equity still gives you more room against drawdown and future losses.

Every approved payout starts a new cycle, and your withdrawable profit resets to $0.
