Payouts

When you can request a payout, payout caps and how payouts affect your account.

  • How payouts work

    Request a payout every 14 days in the funded stage, via Rise or crypto, and keep 80% of your profit.

  • Payout caps

    Each payout cycle has a cap. Profit above it stays in your account as equity.

  • How payouts affect your drawdown

    Requesting a payout locks your drawdown floor at your starting balance. Keep a buffer or the account closes.
