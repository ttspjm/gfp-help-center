Getting started

What Goat Funded Predictions is, account sizes and prices, and how to get funded.

  • What is Goat Funded Predictions?

    Trade prediction markets with Goat Funded Trader: pass one evaluation, then trade a funded account with an 80% profit split.

  • Account sizes and prices

    Goat Funded Predictions comes in three sizes: $25K for $79, $50K for $149 and $100K for $249.

  • How the 1-Step Evaluation works

    Reach the 8% profit target within the drawdown and consistency rules to pass. There is no minimum number of trading days.

  • Do I need to verify my identity?

    Yes. Identity verification (KYC) through Sumsub is required once, before you move to the funded stage.
