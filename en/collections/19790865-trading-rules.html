Trading rules

Drawdown limits, position limits and the other rules for evaluation and funded accounts.

  • Trading rules at a glance

    Every Goat Funded Predictions rule on one page: profit target, drawdown, consistency, position limits and more.

  • Maximum drawdown

    The 3.5% maximum drawdown trails your account at the end of each day and locks at your starting balance.

  • Position and share limits

    You can hold up to 30 open positions at once, and each account size has a maximum number of shares per event.

  • Risk per event limit

    You can only trade events priced at or below 0.85.

  • Trading days and qualifying profit days

    No minimum trading days to pass the evaluation. In the funded stage you need 3 qualifying profit days before each payout.

  • Are bots or automated tools allowed?

    No. Trading with bots or automated tools is not allowed.
