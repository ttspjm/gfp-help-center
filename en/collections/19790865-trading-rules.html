Trading rules

Drawdown limits, position limits and the other rules for evaluation and funded accounts.

  • Trading rules at a glance

    Every GOAT and Premium rule on one page: profit target, drawdown, floating loss, consistency, position limits and payouts.

  • Maximum drawdown

    GOAT uses a 4% drawdown that trails your highest equity and locks at your starting balance. Premium uses a 5% static drawdown.

  • Position and share limits

    You can hold up to 30 open positions at once, and each account size has a maximum number of shares per event.

  • Entry price range

    You can open a position at an entry price between 5¢ and 85¢.

  • Minimum profitable days

    You need 4 profitable days on GOAT or 5 on Premium to pass the evaluation, and the same again before each payout.

  • Are bots or automated tools allowed?

    No. Trading with bots or automated tools is not allowed.

  • What happens if I fail my evaluation?

    Breaking a drawdown limit or the GOAT floating loss limit, or running out of time, fails the evaluation. To try again, buy a new one.

  • Is there an inactivity rule?

    Funded accounts need at least one trade every 15 days on GOAT and every 30 days on Premium. Tell support before a break.

  • Prohibited trading practices

    Market manipulation, account abuse, gambling-style risk and automated trading break the rules.

  • Daily drawdown

    You can lose up to 3% in a day on GOAT or 2.5% on Premium, measured at the 5:00 PM ET reset.

  • Maximum floating loss

    On GOAT, your open positions combined can't lose more than 1% of your current balance. Premium and Starter have no floating loss limit.

  • Rules for the original 1-Step Evaluation

    If you bought a $25K, $50K or $100K evaluation at the original prices of $79, $149 or $249, these rules apply to your account.
