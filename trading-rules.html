Consistency rule

No single trading day can make up more than 35% of your total profit. The rule applies in the evaluation and in the funded stage.

In the funded stage it resets with every approved payout and only counts the profit you make in the new payout cycle.

Example: on a $25K evaluation, reaching the $2,000 profit target means no single day can contribute more than $700.

Maximum drawdown

Your maximum drawdown is 3.5%, trailing on an end-of-day basis. It sets the floor your account must stay above.

Where your floor starts

Account sizeStarting floor
$25K$24,125
$50K$48,250
$100K$96,500

How it trails

The floor is checked at the end of each trading day. When your account closes a day at a new high, the floor moves up with it. The floor never moves down.

When it locks

Once the floor reaches your starting balance, it stops trailing and stays at your starting balance.

Requesting a payout also locks the floor at your starting balance, permanently. After your first payout request, if your account equity reaches or falls below your starting balance, the account is breached.

Keep a buffer. Once the floor is at your starting balance, withdrawing all of your profit leaves nothing above the floor, and the account is closed.

Position and share limits

Open positions

You can hold up to 30 open positions at once across your account.

The limit counts each distinct market and side you hold, and it is the same for every account size. It applies to both evaluation and funded accounts.

Once you have 30 open positions, close one before you open a new one.

Risk per event limit

You can trade events priced at or below 0.85. Markets priced above 0.85 are not eligible.

Trading days and qualifying profit days

Is there a minimum number of trading days?

Not in the evaluation. You pass as soon as you hit the 8% profit target within the drawdown, daily drawdown and consistency rules.

What counts as a trading day

A trading day counts when at least one of your orders fills during that New York calendar day.

Qualifying profit days in the funded stage

Before each payout, you need 3 qualifying profit days in that payout cycle. A qualifying profit day is a day where your realized profit is at least 0.5% of your starting balance.

Are bots or automated tools allowed?

No. Trading with bots or automated tools is not allowed on Goat Funded Predictions.
